Our Story & Craft

More Than a Bakery.
A Place to Gather.

Located in the vibrant heart of Aliganj, Lucknow, The Bakers Farm was founded on a simple passion: to unite the warmth of an artisan European-style bakery with the appetizing flavors of a modern café and casual dining destination.

Every cake sponge is whipped from scratch, our pasta sauces are slow-simmered, and our street-style momos and noodles are wok-tossed hot when you order. No compromises, no shortcuts — just genuine hospitality.
